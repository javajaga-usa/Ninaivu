# The first day

Right after the administrator is made, the console walks through the five
things a new library needs. Each step can be skipped and each is just a page
of the console, so nothing here is a second way of doing anything.

![The first day](../screens/console-first-day.jpg)

## 1. Where are the photographs?

Choose the folder that holds them. Ninaivu only ever reads it: nothing is
moved, renamed or changed. You can add more folders later on **Library
settings**, and sweep old drives and memory cards into one dated archive with
**Import** — which also understands a Google Photos Takeout export, dates,
places, descriptions and albums included.

## 2. Who is in the household?

A **family member** taps their name and enters a PIN. A **guest** signs in
with a password (or a PIN, or a tap, set on **People** later) and sees only
what you make public. Add the people you know of now; the rest can come later
on **People**. A profile without a PIN can be opened from any phone or
computer on your home network, so give a PIN to anyone whose photographs
should stay theirs. [More on roles.](family-and-roles.md)

## 3. What should Ninaivu work out by itself?

Everything here runs on this computer; no photograph leaves it.
**Find the people in photographs** needs a 37 MB model, downloaded when you
turn it on. **Name the places** needs an 11 MB list of places, downloaded
once from GeoNames. **Read the words in photographs** needs the text reader
from Extras. **Search by description** — finding "the beach at sunset"
without anybody tagging it — is about 2 GB, added only if you press *Add
search by description*; it is worth it on a computer with 8 GB of memory or a
graphics card. Each pass takes time on a large library and can be turned off
part-way. [More on AI.](ai.md)

## 4. A copy outside the house

A fire, a theft or a failing disk would take every photograph. **Mugil**
keeps an encrypted copy on Google Drive and checks once a week that it
restores. It needs a Google account and an encryption key — nothing is
uploaded until the key is made — which take a few minutes on the Mugil page;
the Overview reminds you until it is done. [More on backup.](backup.md)

## 5. On the household's phones

Open the address shown on a phone on the home Wi-Fi and add it to the home
screen: it opens like an app. From its backup screen, the photographs and
videos you choose are sent to the house; keep the page open while it sends.

## After that

The console's **Overview** answers two questions before anything else: *is
everything safe?* and *what needs you?* — uploads to approve, faces without a
name, photographs that may be on their side. Come back to it now and then.
