    Turn these the right way up?

    This writes the turn into the files themselves, so they stay the right way up everywhere — copied to a stick, attached to an email, opened in Explorer. Nothing is re-encoded: photographs have two bytes changed and videos are re-wrapped with every frame copied across, so no quality is lost and turning them back restores them exactly.

    An untouched copy of each file is kept in the _deleted/_originals folder in your library the first time Ninaivu writes to it. Anything that could not be turned without re-encoding is left alone and named afterwards.

    Delete these items?

    They are moved to a _deleted folder in your library, filed under today's date. They leave the gallery at once, and you can put them back from the console — or just drag them out in Finder.
